# Doctrines — playtest builds

Playtest builds of **Doctrines**, a single-player grand strategy game on a decaying Ringworld, in development.
This repository holds only the builds. The source is private.

Each build is a release on the [Releases](../../releases) page. Download the zip, unzip it anywhere and run
`Doctrines.exe`. Windows 10/11, 64-bit, no install needed. Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown
publisher: choose *More info* → *Run anyway*. `PLAYTEST.txt` inside has the controls and things worth trying.

## What is in a build

- **A campaign map** of big hexes, each a picture of the country below it: trees, hills, peaks, fields, marsh,
  towns and lakes. Two armies march on each other; where they meet you zoom straight onto that hex's
  battlefield, cut from the same land, with the neighbouring hexes drawn around it. Losses, runaways and
  prisoners carry back to the map.
- **A skirmish** you set up yourself. Build an army on points, design each unit's kit and formation, write its
  doctrine (what it does on its own), pick its drill and captain, deploy, and fight an AI army on a 4.2 km
  hexagonal field with hills, woods, rivers, roads and a camp. Scenarios: pitched battle, encounter, assault on a
  camp by day or night, ambush of a marching column.
- **Orders that travel.** Your orders go by courier, drum and flag, or a paired speaking-stone, so units act on
  their doctrine until the order reaches them. Captains read orders through their own temper and perks, and may
  refuse a stale one.
- **Every soldier simulated and drawn.** Pikes, muskets, bows, crossbows, lances and shields handled as the
  drill books have it; volleys on the officer's word; countermarch, platoon fire and fire-and-advance; men who
  fall, run, rally, make last stands or surrender. Placeholder sound and music from free sources.
- **One control scheme** on every screen, Total War style in battle, with the hotkey on every button and F1 for
  the whole list.
